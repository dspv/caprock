# Talks: your agent and someone else's

A Talk is your agent and another person's agent working something out for
the two of you — a time for a call, a plan to split, notes to swap — while
either of you can read along and type in. Each of you runs your own
Caprock with your own agent; the two Caprocks talk through the relay of
*access from anywhere*, end-to-end encrypted, so the relay sees that two
machines talk and never what.

It is made for people you trust: once you are linked, a new Talk is two
actions away — **Talk** next to their name, then the goal and Enter.

## Link with someone, once

1. **You:** Talks (in the top row, the phone's ⋯ menu, or ⌘K → *Talk
   with…*) → **Invite someone**. Caprock makes a link and copies it:
   `https://caprock.dev/talk#…`. Send it to the one person you mean, as you
   would any message. It works once, for a day; **Withdraw** takes it back.
2. **Them:** Talks → paste the link into *Paste a link someone sent you* →
   **Accept**.
3. Both of you now see each other under **People**, with a twelve-digit
   **safety code** — the same on both sides. Compare it if you want to be
   sure it is them (read it out on a call); it is never a required step.

Inviting or accepting turns *access from anywhere* on, if it was off: that
is how the two Caprocks reach each other. Nothing about your work goes with
the link.

## Start a Talk

**Talk** next to their name, type the goal in one line and press Enter (or
⌘K → *Talk with Ana*). They see the goal. Your agent writes first; then the
agents take turns. Each turn your agent reads the goal, your brief, the
conversation so far and what your 2nd Brain keeps about the goal — it has
no tools in a Talk: it cannot open your files, run anything or reach your
sessions. Memories from folders labelled *Personal* and from anything you
excluded from 2nd Brain are left out.

The conversation reads like a chat with three voices: **your agent** on the
right, **theirs** on the left, and **the two of you** — type into the field
at the bottom and it goes to them and to both agents, who read it on their
next turn.

## Before anything leaves

Every message your side sends — your agent's or yours — goes through the
guard first: keys, tokens and passwords are taken out, and your
**never-share** terms (Settings → Talks) are checked, in any case, anywhere
in the text.

- A message from your agent that names something on your list **waits for
  you**: the hold card shows it *as it would leave*, with that part taken
  out. **Send** sends exactly that, **Edit** lets you write your own
  version, **Drop** sends nothing and lets your agent try again.
- Your agent can also ask you before it goes on; the same card shows its
  note (to you only, never sent).
- A line you type that names a never-share term is not sent at all.

**What left** in the Talk's header lists every message your side sent, word
for word, with when, how (your agent, a hold, you), what was caught and
whether they have it. Nothing else leaves: no files, no folders, no
sessions, no 2nd Brain notes.

## Limits

A Talk stops and asks you when your agent has sent 12 messages or spent
$5 — both are Caprock's defaults, yours to change in Settings → Talks
(*Messages per side*, *Dollars per side*). **Continue** gives it as much
again; **End the Talk** ends it on both sides. The cost line under the
header shows your side's spend and, unless they switched it off, theirs.

## When you are away

A Talk that waits for you — a held message, a cap, a failed turn — sends a
notification to the desktop app and your paired phone, and to Telegram if
you set up a bot there: who it is with and why, never the held message.
Your phone reads every Talk and, with control allowed, answers it — Send,
Edit, Drop, Continue, End, or type in. Inviting, accepting, Remove and
Settings → Talks stay on your computer.

## Remove someone

Talks → **Remove** next to their name → *Remove Ana on both sides*. Their
Caprock is told and forgets you too; your open Talks with them end. Each of
you keeps your own copy of the conversations.

## Settings → Talks

- **Your name in Talks** — what the people you talk with see.
- **Never share** — one term per line: a client, a project, a person. The
  list itself is never sent. It applies at once, to open Talks too.
- **Brief** — what every Talk's agent is told about what not to share; the
  default is *Never share secrets, keys, credentials, or other people's
  private data.*
- **Messages per side**, **Dollars per side** — the caps for new Talks.
- **Tell the other side what a Talk cost you** — on by default.
