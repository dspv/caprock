# Age rating answers (App Store Connect → App Information → Age Rating)

Expected result: **4+**. The app shows the user's own coding-agent sessions
from the user's own computer, and in the demo a fixed set of sample sessions
about code, notes and a weekend run. It has no content of its own beyond
that.

- Violence (cartoon or fantasy, realistic, prolonged graphic or sadistic): **None**
- Sexual content or nudity, graphic sexual content: **None**
- Profanity or crude humor: **None**
- Mature or suggestive themes, horror or fear themes: **None**
- Alcohol, tobacco or drug use or references: **None**
- Simulated gambling, contests: **None**
- Medical or treatment information, health or wellness topics: **None**
- Unrestricted web access: **No** (there is no browser in the app; a tapped
  link opens Safari)
- User-generated content shared with other users: **No** (nothing the user
  writes is shown to anyone else)
- Messaging and chat between people: **No** (the chat is with the user's
  own coding agent on the user's own computer)
- Advertising: **No**
- Parental controls, age assurance: **No**
- Made for Kids: **No**

If App Store Connect asks whether the app shows content generated by an AI
model: **Yes** — the sessions it shows are the user's own agents' output,
produced on and for the user's computer. Answer the follow-up questions about
that content as above; the app adds nothing to it.
